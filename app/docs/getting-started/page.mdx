# Getting Started

## What is RFP Copilot?

RFP Copilot reads your RFP documents, extracts every requirement, builds a compliance matrix and helps you write the proposal response with AI. Your own past documents are used as context, so drafts sound like your company.

## Creating your account

1. Go to the [registration page](/register).
2. Enter your name, email and a password (8+ characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a digit).
3. If you received an invite code (for example during the private beta), enter it in **Invite / promo code** to get the extended 30-day trial.

## Setting up your organization

After registering you are asked to name your organization. It is the workspace for your documents, analyses and proposals. You become its **Owner** and can invite teammates later (see [Team Management](/docs/team)).

## Uploading your first document

1. Open **Documents** in the sidebar.
2. Drag a PDF or Word (.docx) file into the upload area.
3. Wait until the status changes to **Ready** — the text has then been extracted and indexed for search.

## Your first RFP analysis

1. Open **RFP Analyzer** and click **Analyze RFP**.
2. Choose the RFP document you uploaded.
3. Wait about a minute while the AI reads it.
4. Review the extracted requirements, deadlines and evaluation criteria.
5. Click **Generate proposal** to start a draft from the analysis.
