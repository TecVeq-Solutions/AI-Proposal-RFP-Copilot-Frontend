# Team Management

## Inviting members

Open **Settings**, go to the team section and enter your teammate's email address and role. They receive an email with a link to join your organization.

## Roles

| Role | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Full control of the organization, including billing |
| Admin | Manages members and content |
| Member | Creates and edits documents, analyses and proposals |

## Permissions

- Only **Owners** and **Admins** can invite or remove members and change roles.
- All roles can view and work on the organization's documents and proposals.
- Every request is scoped to your organization — other organizations can never see your data.
