# FAQ

## How accurate is the AI?

The AI is a drafting and extraction assistant, not a replacement for review. Requirements are extracted from your document text, but always verify important requirements and generated content before submitting.

## Which file formats are supported?

PDF and Word (.docx) documents.

## Is my data secure?

Documents are stored in encrypted object storage and are only accessible to members of your organization. Access is controlled by role.

## How does billing work?

New accounts start with a free trial (14 days, or 30 days with a beta invite code). No credit card is required to start.

## How do I cancel?

Owners can cancel from **Settings**. Access continues until the end of the current billing period.
